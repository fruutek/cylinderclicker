### What's new in 0.9.0: a factory that clogs - and unclogs

**Your factory is now a real production line.** Cylinders queue on the conveyors, dispatch has limited room, and when it
fills up the whole line backs up behind it - you can watch the jam creep back from Packing, machine by machine. Then you
buy a fix and watch it all start moving again.

- **See it.** Queues of cylinders pile up on the belts. A machine with nowhere to put its cylinder dims and shows a warning;
  its animation stops. When the pad is full a red **PAD FULL** appears.
- **Tapping obeys the line.** Tap a blocked machine and nothing happens (no cylinder, no money, no lost progress); a red
  **LINE FULL** shows over it. Automatic machines pause and carry on by themselves.
- **The van carries a limited load** and the pad holds a limited number of cylinders. While the van is away, cylinders keep
  piling up on the pallets. You are paid as it drives off.
- **Three new ways to fix a jam**, in the Upgrades tab (copper and stainless each have their own): **Bigger Delivery Vans**
  (cylinders per trip - the van grows into a lorry), **Dispatch Storage** (more pallets on the pad, you see them appear) and
  **Faster Turnaround** (the van is back sooner). When the line backs up the shop marks the upgrade that clears it fastest
  in red.
- **Time away respects dispatch.** A fully automated factory earns what its van can carry, and the welcome-back box tells
  you when dispatch held it back.

**Your save is safe.** 0.8.0 saves load unchanged (save version 7). Your van upgrades keep their levels and now do something
real; Dispatch Storage starts at level 0. The first ten minutes of a new game play exactly as before.

**Installing on Android:** this APK is signed with the same shared test key as 0.8.0, so it installs **over 0.8.0** and keeps
your game. (0.4.0 - 0.7.x used different keys: uninstall them first, or use **Transfer Save > Copy save code**, then
**Paste save code** here.)
