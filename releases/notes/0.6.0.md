
### What's new in 0.6.0: Warehouse, Stock Control & Customer Relationships

Making cylinders is no longer the same as selling them. Finished cylinders go into a **warehouse** (racks you can see on the factory floor, growing with each upgrade); customers **telephone** for a product and a quantity and you choose how to answer - part delivery with a backorder, only what is available, a full order later, or decline. Orders ship on the existing lorry and forklift, are paid per lorry, and never twice.

- Persistent customers with relationships, history, patience and price sensitivity; loyalty builds slowly.
- A sales rep with demand forecasts, recommended stock, shortfalls and lost sales (train it for better forecasts).
- Seven products (copper and stainless, indirect / direct / horizontal / slimline) with their own costs and build times, unlocked by specialist machinery.
- Stock targets, priorities and an automatic planner; surplus can go to a wholesaler at a lower price - nothing is ever thrown away.
- Offline: factories keep building to your targets and your sales rep takes the calls.
- Your existing save is migrated automatically (save version 5): money, upgrades, unlocks and open contracts all carry over.
