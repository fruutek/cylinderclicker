### What's new in 0.9.1: you can now SEE the traffic jam

**Cylinders no longer disappear at the end of the conveyor.** In 0.9.0 the dispatch pad existed in the numbers but was drawn
as a faint strip at the bottom of the screen, so finished cylinders seemed to vanish at the belt end. Now:

- **A dispatch yard sits right at the end of the conveyor**, below the van's door. Every finished carton **drops off the belt
  onto a pallet** and the pile of boxes grows while the van is away.
- **When the yard is full** ("PAD FULL") the last conveyor fills with waiting cartons, then Packing stops, then Insulation,
  Pressure Test, Welding, Rolling and Cutting - one machine after another, each one visibly standing still. A belt whose
  cylinders are stuck stops moving.
- **When the van comes back**, boxes hop from the pile onto it and the whole backlog moves forward again.
- **Dispatch Storage** adds pallets to the yard (one per level, up to 12), so you can see the extra room.
- The last conveyor now runs just past its final carton, so nothing hangs off the end.

Nothing about the game itself has changed: the economy, upgrade prices, production speeds and saves are exactly as in 0.9.0.

**Installing on Android:** signed with the same shared test key as 0.8.0 and 0.9.0, so this installs **over 0.8.0 / 0.9.0** and keeps
your game. (0.4.0 - 0.7.x used different keys: uninstall them first, or use **Transfer Save > Copy save code**, then
**Paste save code** here.)
