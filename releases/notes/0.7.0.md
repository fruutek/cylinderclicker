
### What's new in 0.7.0: made for the phone

**Simpler, more visual, more satisfying.** The factory is the star again. Beside it there is one slim deck: how full your warehouse is, *one thing to do* (a customer's order card when somebody is on the phone, otherwise the best next upgrade with a BUY button), and four places to go - **FACTORY | ORDERS | STOCK | UPGRADES**.

- **One-tap orders.** A customer rings: *Wants 10 x Copper Direct - In stock 5 - Missing 5*. Tap **SEND 5 + MAKE 5**, **SEND 5 ONLY** or **DECLINE** (or just **FULFIL ORDER** when it is all in stock). Production, reservations, part deliveries, backorders and lorries are handled for you, and you see how the customer felt: "Customer happy! +3 relationship" / "Delivery late! Customer unhappy."
- **AUTO STOCK** (the default) keeps the shelves right for you; switch to MANUAL if you like to choose. Your sales rep gives plain advice with one-tap buttons: RESTOCK RECOMMENDED, UPGRADE WAREHOUSE, FOCUS ON BACKORDERS.
- **Upgrades, best first**, in plain words. Same upgrades, same prices.
- Big contracts ship by themselves when finished. Fewer interruptions: at most two customers wait, and your rep answers if you do not.
- Every number is still there behind **MORE DETAILS**. Existing saves carry over and keep your stock settings.

Also included - the **warehouse, stock control and customer relationships** expansion (0.6.0, never released separately): a warehouse per factory with racks you can see, seven products with specialist machinery, persistent customers whose relationships grow slowly, part deliveries and backorders, a sales rep with forecasts, and unattended factories that keep selling while you are away. Save version 5; saves from 0.5.x migrate automatically.
