### What's new in 0.8.0: back to the fun

**The factory is the whole game again.** Click, make cylinders, watch the line run, and every few seconds a van drives off and you get paid. No stock shelves, no customer calls, no forecasts - just upgrade, automate and grow.

- **Choose what to make.** Six copper cylinder types (Standard, Direct, Indirect, Slimline, Horizontal, Twin-Coil) and six stainless ones (up to the Premium Twin-Coil). Each is unlocked once with a **New Product** upgrade, then picked on the new **CYLINDERS** tab - or just tap **NOW MAKING**. Each type is made differently on screen (colour and shape on the belt), takes a bit longer and pays more.
- **The delivery van.** Finished cylinders wait on the pad, the van loads, drives away - and you are paid as it leaves. It never slows the factory. **Faster Vans** and **Bigger Vans** upgrades shorten the round trip, enlarge the load, and the van grows into a lorry.
- **Bonus orders.** Now and then a customer offers an order: tap **ACCEPT** and the factory makes it by itself. Ignore it and nothing bad happens.
- **Simple phone screen.** Cash, what you are making, output, factory switch, menu. Big buttons, nothing overlapping.
- **Factory naming fixed on Android.** The name box now starts empty (the current name shows as a hint), so you get the full 24 characters, spaces included.

**Your save is safe.** Every older save loads. If you had stock on the warehouse shelves, unfinished contract orders, or the retired upgrades, they are paid out as cash once (you will see a message), and any tooling you bought unlocks the matching cylinder.

**Installing on Android:** this build is signed with the project's shared test key (see the README). Android will not update 0.4.0 - 0.7.x in place: uninstall the old app first, or use **Transfer Save** in 0.7.x to export your factory. From now on test-key builds update each other normally.

The full warehouse / customer-relationship game remains available as release 0.7.0.
